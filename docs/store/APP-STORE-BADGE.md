# App Store download badge

`public/store-badges/download-on-the-app-store.svg` is Apple's unmodified
black “Download on the App Store” artwork. Its logo and lettering are vector
paths, so installed fonts cannot change its appearance. The artwork uses the
original English text; the link's accessible label follows the website language.
